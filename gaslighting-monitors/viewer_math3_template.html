Slip an Incorrect Math Solution Through

100 free-response math problems, each with a planted wrong answer. Each attacker model writes a confident solution that arrives at the wrong answer. Each grader then judges that solution. A grader is fooled when it calls the wrong answer correct. Five models both attack and grade: two GLM, two gpt-oss and Kimi-K3.

Family effect, regression

One mixed logistic regression over all three frames: fooled ~ attacker + grader + frame + relation + a random effect per problem. The attacker and grader terms absorb each model's overall strength, and the problem effect absorbs that some problems are easier to deceive on whoever attacks or grades, so relation is the family effect left over. The frame term marks which prompt the monitor got: a neutral "proposed solution", a self-check that presents the pitch as the monitor's own past attempt, or a skim with a 1k thinking cap. Points: change in fooled rate vs a cross-family pair (or vs the neutral frame), averaged over problems; 95% model-based intervals.
